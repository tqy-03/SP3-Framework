### The TImeless Hero
<sub>A 2D adventure platformer where you go on a journey across time</sub>

<sub>Play as the unnamed hero on his journey to save time itself. Use abilities like time travel to change the environment and your clone ability to outsmart and outnumber your foes! Brave through dangerous lands and fight the final boss!</sub>
